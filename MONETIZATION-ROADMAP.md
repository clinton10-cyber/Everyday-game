# Everyday-game — Monetization Roadmap

## Recommended model: free-to-play, fair-play

Keep the main life simulation free. Monetize optional cosmetics, convenience, and substantial new content. Do not sell combat damage, stronger guns, exclusive survival advantages, or guaranteed wins.

## Revenue streams

1. **Cosmetic store** — outfits, wings, emotes, dance packs, vehicle wraps, phone themes, house décor, profile frames and animation effects. Clearly show the exact item before purchase.
2. **Season pass** — a free track plus an optional paid track with cosmetic rewards and missions. Publish the reward list and season duration before sale.
3. **Optional rewarded ads** — players choose to watch an ad for a small, capped reward. Never interrupt driving, combat, dialogue, or a purchase flow with forced ads.
4. **Remove-ads purchase** — one-time purchase that removes forced ads while leaving optional rewarded ads opt-in.
5. **Expansion packs** — paid new districts, story chapters, vehicles, interiors, festivals and career packs. Existing base-game content remains playable.
6. **Creator collaborations** — licensed fashion, music and dance packs with clear permission and revenue-sharing agreements.

## Suggested launch pricing to test (not final)

- Cosmetic mini-pack: ₦500–₦1,500
- Premium outfit / wing / emote bundle: ₦1,000–₦3,000
- Monthly cosmetic pass: ₦2,000–₦3,500
- District or story expansion: ₦3,000–₦7,500
- Remove forced ads: ₦1,500–₦3,000 one-time

Test prices with players before launch. Offer regional pricing and display the final amount before payment.

## Payments and fraud protection

- Use a reputable payment provider with a server-side checkout and verified webhooks.
- Never trust a payment-success message sent by the browser or mobile client.
- Verify the provider transaction on the server, check amount/currency/reference, and process each payment reference only once.
- Grant the purchased entitlement only after server verification; store receipts and an audit trail.
- Provide restore-purchases and support/refund instructions.
- Do not store card details in Everyday-game.

## Economy separation

Everyday Pay's in-game balance and game credits are fictional gameplay currencies. They must not be represented as bank deposits, cash, investment returns, or withdrawable money. Real-money purchases should unlock clearly described digital items or expansions. Keep a visible distinction between in-game earnings and real-money checkout.

## Launch sequence

1. Measure retention and identify the most-used cosmetics.
2. Launch a small cosmetic store and optional rewarded ads.
3. Add a transparent season pass after content cadence is reliable.
4. Add expansions after the base game is stable.
5. Add real payments only after server-side verification, privacy, age-rating, consumer-protection and platform-store requirements have been reviewed.

## Metrics to monitor

Daily/monthly active players, day-1/day-7/day-30 retention, session length, cosmetic conversion, average revenue per paying user, ad opt-in rate, refund rate, failed payments, chargebacks and crash rate. Avoid aggressive monetization that damages retention or player trust.
