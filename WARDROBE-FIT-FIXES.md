# Wardrobe fit fixes (faceted look)
- Shoulder caps flattened and lowered: sleeves/skin shoulders no longer stand up like pauldrons.
- Leggings now sit outside the leg (skin no longer shows through); cloth knee added so knees stay covered.
- Palazzo flares smoothly from the hip instead of a boxy block.
- Dashiki/kaftan/agbada: trouser waistband no longer pokes through the tunic.
