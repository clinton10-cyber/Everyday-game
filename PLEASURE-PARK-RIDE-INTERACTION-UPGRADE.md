# Pleasure Park – Interactive Ferris Wheel & Roller Coaster

- Rebuilt the Ferris wheel with a central axle, structural supports, reinforced rim, 16 spoke assemblies, and 12 upright gondolas.
- The wheel rotates continuously while gondolas counter-rotate to remain upright.
- Added a boarding interaction that takes the player on a timed panoramic Ferris wheel ride.
- Rebuilt the roller coaster with a closed elevated track, twin rails, cross sleepers, support columns, and a three-car train with seats and safety bars.
- Added a boarding interaction that runs a first-person camera ride around the track.
- Player movement and NPC simulation pause during the ride; the player returns to a nearby exit platform when the ride ends.
- Service worker cache advanced to v132 and precache regenerated.

Validation: all JavaScript files pass `node --check`; ZIP integrity checked. Browser/game runtime testing remains outstanding.
