# Phase 6 — Relationships and Family

Phase 6 builds on the relationship and life-simulation systems already present in the Phase 5 project. The existing dating, engagement, wedding, pregnancy, childbirth, parenting, family companion, outfit, and photo-album systems are retained.

## Existing systems retained
- Meet residents, build friendships, date, become engaged, and marry.
- Plan a wedding and complete the ceremony in the church.
- Pregnancy progression, antenatal visits, childbirth, and adoption.
- Children grow over game time; infants can be carried, soothed, and put down.
- Call spouse and children to accompany the player in supported scenes.
- Choose family members' outfits and capture family/date/wedding photos.
- People and Life phone apps provide access to relationship and household information.

## Phase 6 additions
- Expanded Family tab with household overview, partner status, children, and relationship strength.
- Persistent household happiness meter and family-activity counter.
- Four family activities: free game night, park picnic (₦3,500), family dinner (₦9,000), and family celebration (₦18,000).
- Completing an activity increases family happiness, improves relationship scores with the spouse/partner and children, and improves their mood.
- Activity progress and the most recent activity are saved locally.
- Insufficient funds are checked before paid activities; failed purchases do not change family progress.

## Testing notes
- JavaScript syntax checks and ZIP integrity checks are run for this package.
- Full 3D gameplay, device performance, and persistence across an actual app restart should still be tested on target Android/desktop devices.
