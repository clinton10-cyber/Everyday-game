Everyday in PH — Free Port Harcourt Open World Game

  

    Everyday in PH is a free open-world browser game set in Port Harcourt,
    Nigeria. Walk the streets, drive cars, take jobs, buy a home, and survive
    the Garden City. No install, no download — it runs in any modern browser
    on phone or desktop.
  

  

    Prefer an app? Download Everyday in PH for Android (APK) or Windows (EXE)
    from the official download page.
  


  
What you can do in Everyday in PH

  

    Everyday in PH is a free Nigerian open-world game and city life simulator built
    for the browser. Explore Port Harcourt in 3D, drive cars and bikes, buy houses and
    furniture, work jobs, and visit interiors like the market, hospital, church, mosque,
    hotel, and government house. Citizens live their own lives: they jog, sit on the
    ground, bless their food and eat, hug, shake hands and lean on tables — and so can you.
    Watch your sanity bar and reputation bar: let your sanity climb too high and your
    character can stop obeying the controls.
  


  
How to play

  

    Drag the left half of the screen to walk and the right half to look around.
    Tap the action button to interact with people, doors, cars, and items.
    Open the phone in the top-right corner to check your balance, jobs, and map.
    In a car, tap the pedals to accelerate and brake; drag the screen to steer.
  


  
Tips

  
    
    
  • Start with simple jobs at the Job Center before attempting higher-risk contracts.
    • 
    
  • Keep your pocket bag empty — you only have 8 slots and some pickups are worth reselling.
    • 
    
  • Watch the alert meter at the top of the player card. Above 70%, police will start searching for you.
    • 
  


  
Frequently asked questions

  
Is Everyday in PH free to play?

  
Yes. The game is completely free to play in your browser. No download or account signup is required to try it.

  
Does it work on mobile?

  
Yes. Everyday in PH is built mobile-first and works on iOS and Android browsers. Touch controls are optimized for one-thumb play.

  
How is progress saved?

  
Your progress is stored locally in your browser. Clearing browser data or switching devices will reset your profile.
