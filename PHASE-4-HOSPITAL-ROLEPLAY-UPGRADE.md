# Phase 4 — Walkable Hospital Roleplay

- Added a Visit City Hospital action to the phone Health app.
- The Health app now routes into the hospital's walkable 3D interior.
- Added reception/triage, doctor consultation and pharmacy interaction points.
- Triage records local visit count; doctor treatment restores up to 65 HP for ₦7,500; pharmacy sells a medical kit for ₦3,000.
- Medical kits are added to the existing local inventory and can be used through the phone Health app.
- All services use simulated in-game naira only. No real-world medical service or payment is involved.
