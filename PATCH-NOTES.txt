Everyday-game patch

Changed files:
- css/hud.css
  * Reflows the mobile landscape combat/context controls into a compact two-column cluster.
  * Keeps the control dock above the bottom thumb controls and reduces button height/spacing.
- js/scenes/shootingrange.js
  * Simplifies the dedicated range weapon/skill dock with consistent sizing, spacing, and landscape sizing.
- js/core/guns.js
  * Closes the support-hand fingers while firearms are drawn and releases them when holstered.
  * Adjusts support-arm pose values for rifle, SMG, shotgun, and LMG.

Install:
Copy these files into the matching paths in your project, replacing the existing files.

Validation:
JavaScript syntax checks passed for the modified JS files. This patch has not been visually tested in the running game. The support-hand pose is improved, but exact hand-to-foregrip contact still needs an in-game check against your character rig and weapon proportions.
