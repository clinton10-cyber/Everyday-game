# Wings & Naija Trend Dance Upgrade

## New cosmetics
- Celestial Angel Wings — 18,000 game credits
- Shadow Raven Wings — 22,000 game credits
- Neon Pulse Wings — 26,000 game credits

Wings are attached to the third-person avatar after purchase, flap subtly while the world runs, and are reattached when the player rebuilds their character in the wardrobe.

## New dance pack
Naija Trend Dance Pack — 18,000 game credits. Unlocks five original procedural routines inspired by popular dance trends:
- Azonto-inspired step
- Shaku flow
- Zanku legwork
- Buga bounce
- Amapiano log-drum groove

These are original game animations inspired by broad dance styles, not copied music-video choreography. The routines are available from the open-world Actions menu under “Naija trend dances”. The shop checks ownership before a locked routine can play.

## Persistence
Purchases use the existing game economy and are saved in the existing local style-shop ownership record. They are not real-money purchases.


## Steppers Dance Expansion
- Added three original procedural Steppers-inspired emotes: Steppers Glide, Cross-Step Bounce, and Heel-Toe Switch.
- Added all three to the Naija dance action menu and gated them behind the existing Naija Trend Dance Pack.
- The routines use the existing character rig and animation controller; no external dance video or copyrighted choreography is bundled.
