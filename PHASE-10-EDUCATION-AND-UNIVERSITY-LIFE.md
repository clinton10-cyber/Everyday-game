# Phase 10 — Education and University Life

Adds an in-game Education application with enrolment in primary, secondary and university programmes, course lists, study sessions, lecture attendance, examinations, credits, scholarships and graduation. Academic progress is stored locally on the device. Tuition and scholarship transactions use the existing fictional in-game economy.

## Phone Comic Book addition
Adds Everyday Comics to the phone with all 30 original concepts supplied by the player, organised into six genres. Players can open story summaries and short sample story panels, save favourites and retain reading progress locally.

## Testing notes
JavaScript syntax and ZIP integrity checks are included in the release process. Full visual and gameplay testing should be completed in a browser/device before public release.
