# Origami wings (v1.3.9)
The three wing designs in Emotes & Gear / Wardrobe are replaced with folded-ribbon wings (`js/characters/origami-wings.js`):
- Origami Angel Wings (`angel_wings`) – ivory paper feathers, ink creases, warm core
- Cyber Origami Wings (`neon_wings`) – dark chrome, magenta→cyan neon edges, energy conduit, spinning core
- Shadow Raven Wings (`shadow_wings`) – obsidian plumage in six feather tiers, violet/teal iridescent edges
Prices and saved ownership/equipped ids are unchanged. Geometry and materials are built once per design and shared across avatars.
Wings hang from the shoulder blades (feathers fan down, wrist up) and flap gently; hidden while lying, falling or flipping as before.
