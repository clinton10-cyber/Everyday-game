# Phone UI redesign audit — after Phase 11

## Unique interface passes now applied
- Camera: camera-focused viewfinder, modes, shutter and selfie pose presentation.
- Gallery: dark media-library layout, photo grid and 3D album entry.
- Map: navigation-themed launch surface connected to the existing full-screen MapApp.
- Weather: Port Harcourt seasonal forecast interface.
- Music: soundscape controls connected to the existing AudioSystem (not a pretend music-streaming service).
- Inventory: pocket-storage grid, capacity display and item inspection.
- Shop: retail identity and category browsing, preserving existing purchase logic.
- Police: dispatch/control-room presentation, preserving dispatch and crime log.
- Health: clinical health dashboard, preserving HP, medkit and hospital actions.
- Players: City People directory sourced from actual spawned NPCs; explicitly not online human players.
- Hunt: contract-board visual identity, preserving contracts, reputation, cash-out, leaderboard and achievements.
- Settings and About: separate system and game-information visual surfaces.

## Further functional depth still possible
1. Camera exposure/zoom and richer capture review.
2. Gallery album filtering, favourites and metadata.
3. Map route preview and destination search within MapApp.
4. Weather hourly forecast and stronger live-world condition synchronisation.
5. A licensed/local music catalogue only if actual audio tracks are added; current controls manage ambience.
6. Inventory item-specific actions only where the underlying item system supports them.
7. Shop product previews and deeper checkout details.
8. Police report submission and case tracking beyond the existing dispatch/crime log.
9. Health treatment history and appointments connected to actual hospital data.
10. NPC profile detail pages and social links using real NPC character models.
11. Hunt contract artwork and richer progress telemetry.
12. Settings controls for additional actual device/game settings.

The app-specific interfaces are a visual and interaction foundation, not a claim that every proposed future feature is already implemented.
