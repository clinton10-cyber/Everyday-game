# v1.3.16 - Caps & necklaces fixed, many new head / neck accessories

## Fixes
- Caps: the peak was built on the wrong half of the head (a thin sliver on the right side). Now a proper curved front peak, band and button. Crown raised so it no longer covers the eyebrows.
- Hair fringe no longer pokes through any hat (faceted look).
- Necklaces: the chain was a floating, upside-down arc that sank into the collar. Necklaces are now closed loops that sit on the neck and drape onto the chest, following the measured torso surface (male / female / neutral).
