# Phase 3 — Walkable Courthouse & Bank Top-ups

- Added a Rivers State High Court landmark and a dedicated walkable 3D courtroom interior.
- Justice phone app now includes Visit Courthouse.
- The courtroom includes judge bench, counsel tables, witness stand, public gallery seating, clerk registry, court staff/participants and a physical hearing interaction.
