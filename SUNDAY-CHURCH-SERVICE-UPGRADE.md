# Sunday Church Service Upgrade

- Uses the in-game life-simulation calendar (day 6 of each 7-day cycle) and game clock.
- From 08:00 to 13:29 on Sundays, 38 congregation NPCs occupy the church pew seats and face the pulpit.
- A pastor stands at the pulpit and loops a dedicated preaching gesture.
- An usher remains standing near the entrance and gives a welcoming wave.
- Congregation NPCs are removed and seats released when the Sunday service window ends or the church scene is closed.
- The church scene now ticks its own NPCs so the congregation and preacher animations continue while inside.

Validation: JavaScript syntax checks and ZIP integrity checks. Browser/game runtime testing has not yet been performed.
