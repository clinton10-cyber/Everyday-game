# Everyday-game — Limited Fashion & Garden City Connect Upgrade

## Limited fashion shop
- Added a Limited Fits tab to the phone Shop.
- Includes Royal Agbada, Neon Runner, Shadow Ninja, Orbit Explorer and Angelic Limited Dress.
- Purchases use the existing in-game Economy balance.
- Purchased outfits are saved locally and can be re-equipped without paying again.
- Outfit application uses the existing Wardrobe system so existing avatar customisation remains intact.

## Garden City Connect social app
- Rebuilt the existing Social app with For You, Following and People tabs.
- Player posts can include photos selected from the in-game Gallery.
- Post and like actions consume simulated mobile data.
- NPC residents create city-moment posts from the live game scene while the Social app is open.
- NPC posts are generated periodically and use NPC life names/roles where available.
- Added verified account badges for official-style and public-service NPC accounts, including Garden City Updates.
- Players can follow/unfollow accounts, like posts and write comments.
- NPC reactions can add likes, comments and followers to player posts.
- Social feed, follows, reactions and comments are saved in local browser storage.
- Attached photos are resized/compressed before being saved to keep the feed lightweight.

## Notes
- Social interactions are simulated single-player NPC behaviour; they do not connect to a real online multiplayer server.
- Limited collection prices and stock labels are balancing values and can be adjusted later.
