# Phase 14 — Pleasure Park Open World & UNIPORT Campus Expansion

## Pleasure Park
- Expanded the standalone park into a broad, outdoor, walkable environment.
- Increased the movement boundary to cover the landscaped grounds.
- Added a connected promenade network and perimeter walking loop.
- Added an ornamental pond, planted lawns, flower beds, benches and park lighting.
- Added outdoor Ferris wheel, roller-coaster track, carousel, splash pools, open-air amphitheatre and food kiosks.
- Preserved the existing attraction buildings and their interaction points as indoor venues within the larger park.

## University of Port Harcourt
- Added an architectural detail pass to all existing campus buildings: individual facade accents, glazed entrance doors, steps, entrance portals, window reveals, trim bands, planters and distinctive building-specific features.
- Added additional campus buildings for Management Sciences, Agriculture, Education, Pharmaceutical Sciences, Dentistry, Communication & Media Studies and Student Hostels.
- Preserved the existing campus road network, building interiors, collision model, player controls and drive zones.
