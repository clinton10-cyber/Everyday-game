# Wings flight (v1.3.9)
Equipped wings (Origami Angel, Cyber Origami, Shadow Raven) now give true flight, faster than the jetpack:
- Works without owning the jetpack; the FLY button / F key shows WINGS and toggles flight, hold JUMP / Space to climb.
- No fuel: wings never run out. The jetpack model is hidden while wings are equipped.
- Speed: x7.5 walking speed in flight (jetpack x2.8); in the drive-scene walker 9x / 14x running (jetpack 5x / 7.5x). Climb up to 22 m/s (jetpack 12-14).
- Wings stay visible while descending and flap faster (and wider) in flight.
- Unequip the wings and the jetpack behaves exactly as before.
Tuning lives in `WING_FLIGHT` in `js/core/style-ability-shop.js`.
